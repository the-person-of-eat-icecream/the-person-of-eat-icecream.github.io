# WAPR article images

Images provided by the blog author for the WAPR reproduction article.

- `tray-pose.png`: pose-estimation overlay on the author's RealSense tray capture.
- `screw-false-positives.png`: screw-scene overlay showing background false matches.

The images illustrate individual experiments and do not establish aggregate pose accuracy. Their filenames and captions correspond to `content/wapr-reproduction.md`.
